    About Us

    In the heart of the bustling city of Valenzuela, Philippines, lies the humble beginnings of Lawang Bato National High School (LBNHS), a beacon of education and community empowerment. Established in 1975, LBNHS emerged from the collective vision of local leaders and educators to provide quality education accessible to all, regardless of socio-economic background.

    Over the decades, LBNHS has produced countless graduates who have gone on to make significant contributions to society, both locally and globally. From accomplished professionals in various fields to civic leaders and entrepreneurs, the alumni of LBNHS continue to carry forward the school’s legacy of excellence and service.
